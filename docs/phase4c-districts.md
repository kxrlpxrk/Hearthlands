# Phase 4C: District designer

## What is stored

Districts remain authoritative **cell sets** in document v3. Optional `editVersion: 1`, `type`, `locked`, `visible` and polygon `boundary` fields extend the existing district objects. A polygon is rasterised at tile centres; its cells drive rendering, membership and area. Painting removes the polygon cache and retains the edited cells. Empty districts in old projects need no migration. Missing type, visibility and lock mean Custom, visible and unlocked. Legacy colours/cells are retained on import; geometry queries normalise legacy coordinates without rewriting their saved data. New editable boundaries use unique in-bounds integer cells and six-digit hex colours. Choose a colour before editing geometry of a legacy district with a named CSS colour.

Overlay settings are optional versioned `districtView` metadata. IndexedDB, snapshots, JSON and history retain these fields without a database replacement or settlement identity change. District edits never constrain building placement, terrain brushes, roads or procedural generation.

## Detection and editing

**Districts → Detect districts · preview** performs deterministic multi-source land growth around planning centres and building uses. It creates civic, harbour, workshop, farm, residential, and (in larger appropriate settlements) market/military areas. Planning open spaces and actual footprints bound the developed land and yards. Water is excluded; tiny isolated fragments are left unclaimed. It changes only districts after **Confirm detected districts**. Confirmation explicitly replaces existing districts; locked districts block replacement. Cancel preserves every existing district. Detection is never triggered automatically by loading or editing a project.

Polygon tools support tapping corners, dragging handles, inserting corners near an edge when reshaping an existing polygon, and deleting a selected corner. A painted/detected district can be reshaped by drawing a replacement polygon, or extended/shrunk with paint/erase. **Preview district boundary** validates crossings and bounds. Brush release produces a preview. **Confirm district edit** commits one undo command. Cancel or Escape discards drafts/previews; a second finger cancels the active stroke/handle drag and navigates the camera. Explicit Pan/Resume preserves previously placed polygon corners. Switching projects or restore points is blocked until district previews/drafts are confirmed or cancelled.

New/edited boundaries cannot take another district's cells, including hidden and locked districts. Conflicting cells appear red. **Apply only unclaimed cells** explicitly clips the proposed edit, preserving existing districts. Imported overlapping districts are preserved; metadata changes remain possible. Repainting/reshaping provides the same overlap review to resolve their geometry.

Buildings belong to the district with the greatest intersection area with their actual rotated, possibly fractional footprint. Ties use district ID, so membership is unique and deterministic; fully outside buildings are unassigned. A boundary change does not move anything. Statistics show member type counts, total cell area, exact union of occupied footprint portions, and remaining unoccupied area. Available area is not a promise of buildability: water, slopes, vegetation and other objects still matter. Highlighting outlines member buildings without modifying selection, locks or positions.

Each district has name/type/colour controls, visibility, lock, duplicate (a separate nearby non-overlapping location), and delete. Locked districts permit hide/show and unlock. Duplication reports lack of space rather than displacing another area. Global overlays, labels and opacity are adjustable. Rendering places overlays below roads and buildings. PNG has independent district fill/boundary and district-name checkboxes; names-only exports are supported. Individual and layer visibility still apply.

## iPhone acceptance steps

1. Open **Projects**, duplicate Ravenfjord, and open the copy. In **Restore points**, create “Before districts”. Keep a JSON backup on another device if needed; browser storage is local.
2. Tap **Districts** in the bottom navigation (scroll the navigation horizontally to reach later tools). Tap **Detect districts · preview**. Inspect the five main regions on the reference Viking river map: civic, harbour, workshops and two residential areas. Tap **Confirm detected districts**. If any existing district is locked, cancel and unlock it first only if you intend to replace it.
3. Open Districts again. Select Farming / agriculture, tap **Draw new polygon**, then **Pan district camera**. Fit/pinch/pan to open land; tap **Resume district editing**. Tap at least three distinct corners around the new farming area. Drag a gold handle to adjust it. Tap **Preview district boundary**, then **Confirm district edit**. Cancel should leave no new area.
4. Choose an existing district in the list. Rename it and press **Rename district**. Choose its type and colour. Tap **Reshape boundary**: existing polygon handles can move, a tap near an edge inserts a corner, and tapping a handle selects it for **Delete selected corner**. Detected/painted areas instead start a new replacement polygon. Preview and confirm the replacement.
5. Use **Extend with brush** or **Shrink with eraser**, set brush size, and drag one finger. Release, review and confirm. Paint across another district to see red conflicts; cancel or explicitly apply only unclaimed cells. Undo once must revert the entire confirmed stroke; Redo restores it.
6. Start a brush stroke, add a second finger and pinch. The active stroke must disappear without a saved change. Try **Pan district camera** and **Resume district editing**; neither should paint.
7. Select the civic/residential district, inspect counts and area, and tap **Highlight district buildings**. Verify highlighted footprints rather than just building origins. Buildings straddling areas have one owner. Clear highlights when finished.
8. **Lock district** and verify boundary, rename, colour and delete controls are disabled. Hide/show the individual district; toggle **Show district overlays** off to inspect the normal map, then on. Try names and opacity. Unlock only when further edits are needed.
9. Wait for **Saved on this device**, close and reopen the browser, and inspect districts on the same project. Restore “Before districts” through **Projects → Restore points → Restore → Confirm restore**, checking the automatic backup exists. Restore that backup to recover the edited districts. Import an exported JSON as a separate project and verify IDs, cells and settings.
10. Open **Export**, download JSON, then prepare/download a 2048 px full-map PNG. Test district overlays on with names off; then overlays off with names on. Pan/zoom must not crop the exported map. In Safari, use Downloads / Files or Share → Save to Files if the image opens. Existing terrain, roads, bridges and buildings must still appear. Use 1024 px if device image memory is limited.

## Verification and limitations

Automated model coverage includes both 35-building presets, cross-seed forest/plains/coastal cases (20–60 buildings), deterministic detection, contiguous non-overlapping land regions, polygon validation, corner editing, brush buffering, overlap clipping, locks/visibility, exact footprint membership/area, history, legacy JSON, autosave/reopen, project isolation, named restore points and SVG/PNG scene options. The full suite also exercises existing generation, infrastructure, terrain and building behaviour.

Browser checks use actual touch events at 390 × 844: duplicate legacy Ravenfjord, detection preview/confirmation, farming polygon, handle drag, rename, paint confirmation, pinch cancellation, locks, overlays, highlighting, undo/redo, JSON and full-map PNG downloads, autosave and actual browser restart. Production-bundle checks additionally exercise overlap cancellation, locked detection replacement, corner insertion/deletion, snapshot restore and independent PNG controls. These checks use Chromium mobile emulation; physical iPhone Safari is not available in this environment. Run the steps above on the device before calling Safari verified.

Boundaries are tile-based rather than freeform vectors. Detection is a planning heuristic, not a cadastral survey, and areas may include vegetation/slopes or divide buildings between neighbours; refine them manually. Tiny remote fragments can remain unassigned. A district can contain disconnected cells when deliberately painted that way. No district regeneration, expansion, or building movement is performed. SVG/PNG full-map image limits and browser storage limits remain unchanged.

Completed checks: `npm test` — **144 tests passed across 10 files**, including 17 district tests; `npm run build` — passed. Vite retains the existing main-bundle size warning (about 803 kB before gzip). Desktop regression verification also designed and edited a 20-building village with single-command drag history, rotation, sizing, rename, locks, duplication/deletion and bounds/collision warnings.
